The brand in one breath

Desi protein for Indian food.

Heldi is a whey protein isolate blend that disappears into dal, curry, sabzi and raita, so the meals a UK desi family already cooks quietly carry more protein. No shaker, no new habits, no separate “healthy” cooking.

Name

/hel-dee/, adj. how my nani says “healthy.” The founder’s nani’s pronunciation, adopted as the brand. This line runs verbatim on the hero and the pack.

Slogan

They shake, we stir. A Bond allusion, and it is locked: ticker, footer, meta description, Organization schema.

Tagline family

“The same food, just a little Heldier.” · “Bringing something new to the table.” · “Same recipes. Same taste. More protein.”

Who we are talking to

UK desi households, roughly 20 to 60, cooking Indian food at home. Second-generation buyers sorting out their parents, and the parents, aunties and uncles themselves. Always British English, always UK reference points.

What this document is

The visual half of the brand: colour, type, shape, motion and imagery, plus a short voice reminder. It is a snapshot for sharing.

The authority is BRAND.md in the repo, and the live component board is docs/brand/specimen.html, which links the production stylesheet. If those two disagree with this PDF, they win.

The five pillars, in one line each

  • Radical honesty about food numbers. We concede the point before we sell.
  • Food first, supplements second. The part of milk that has always been there.
  • The family table. One pot, everyone eats, nobody cooks twice.
  • Desi pride without the stigma. Not gym-boy, not wellness.
  • Proof over promise. Grams, labels and paper trails.

Colour · 3.2 the three schemes

Cream, ink, gold. Repeat.

Colour is applied by the section, never by the element. Pages alternate down the scroll so no two neighbours share a background, and where two colours meet they are separated by a 3px ink top border or the signature double rule.

Cream · the default

Cook like always.

Body copy sits in brown on cream. Cards go white or cream with a 3px ink border. This is where most explaining happens.

Ink · the pause

Here is a normal day, weighed honestly.

Headings in cream, body in dark muted, accents and numbers in marigold. Used for the truth beats and the closing call to action.

Marigold · the welcome

They shake, we stir.

Ink headings, warm dark sublines. The hero and the joyful moments. Terracotta is not legible here, so it never carries text on gold.

The double rule

7px tall, border-block: 3px solid var(--ink). It is the brand’s punctuation mark between two coloured bands. The lighter alternative is a single 3px ink top border.

Rhythm

Vertical padding clamp(3.5rem, 8vw, 6rem). Content maxes at 1152px, FAQ at 860px, story prose at 640px.

Two layouts, always

Every surface ships a deliberate mobile layout (up to 899px) and a deliberate wide layout (900px and up), sharing one DOM and sized with clamp() between them. Structure changes at that single breakpoint and nowhere else.

“The same thing but narrower” is not a mobile design. Check 375px and 1280px before calling anything finished.

Two rules that fall out of the table

Terracotta never carries text on marigold. On a gold section, a hover state swaps the background rather than the text colour.

Muted is for large or secondary text only. If a line in muted has to be read, it goes brown.

The focus ring is white on purpose

outline: 3px solid #fff; outline-offset: 3px, and terracotta on inputs and option cards. Ink and gold sections both need the white ring to show a keyboard user where they are. Do not restyle it away.

Type

Rozha One speaks. Gelasio explains.

One pouch, 25 servings, 10g

Rozha One, weight 400 only. The Indian-inflected editorial serif that makes numbers feel like a menu. It sets h1 and h2, menu card titles, stat numerals, gram pills, the FAQ plus and minus glyphs and the gifting code chip. There is no second weight: if it needs to feel heavier, it needs to be bigger.

Same recipes. Same taste. More protein.

Gelasio 400, 500, 600 and italic, fallback Georgia. Everything else, including nav, buttons and interface. Italic is reserved for the pronunciation line, captions, the founder quote, story notes and the hero subline.

No sans-serif anywhere

Site, email, pack, deck or ad: two families only. A sans-serif slipping into a marketing asset is the fastest way to make Heldi look like every other supplement brand.

Shape language

Hard offset shadows. Never blur.

The signature is an ink-bordered rounded card sitting on a solid offset shadow, as if it were a sticker on a menu. Every shadow is a flat colour at a fixed offset. A blurred shadow anywhere on a Heldi surface is a bug.

Solid buttons are ink with gold text at weight 600, pill radius by default and 8px when square. Hover fills terracotta, turns the text cream and lifts the button 2px. Nothing scales, nothing bounces.

Motion

  • House easing cubic-bezier(0.23, 1, 0.32, 1), 340 to 640ms, always translate plus fade.
  • Canon effects: the 30s linear ticker marquee, the dissolve-in text that evokes powder in liquid (opacity, blur and translate at 55ms per letter), the hero elephant curtain, the spoonful burst in the stir gallery, and the 2px hover lift.
  • Every animation respects prefers-reduced-motion. The codebase already pauses video, skips the curtain and disables smooth scroll. Match that bar or do not ship the animation.

Elephant and wordmark

  • Decorated Indian elephant line illustration, always ink, always ornamental. It flanks the hero, brackets the final call to action and sits mirrored in the nav chip.
  • No faces, no speech, no mascot behaviour. One motif, reused.
  • The HELDI wordmark is a supplied PNG with a slight forward lean. Never redraw it. On ink it turns gold through the .heldi-logo--on-dark filter.

Photography and illustration

Real kitchens, warm light, recognisable food.

Do

  • Real UK desi home kitchens, shared meals, hands serving.
  • Warm colour grade toward marigold and cream.
  • Natural light or warm tungsten.
  • Recognisable dishes: dal, kadhi, raita, chaat.

Do not

  • Shaker bottles, gym settings, scoops on grey seamless.
  • Cold blue grading.
  • “Exotic India” stock tropes.
  • Faces on the elephant, or the elephant behaving like a mascot.

Division of labour

Illustration is always ink line art and covers elephants, icons and infographics. Photography covers food, pouch and family. The two do not swap jobs.

Current product shots are AI-generated placeholders waiting on real photography, tracked in NEXT_STEPS.md.

Voice, in short

Two registers. Never mixed inside one section.

Voice A, the honest expert

Plain, precise, quietly warm. Explains numbers without condescension, concedes points against itself, cites sources. No jokes inside medical, nutrition or compliance copy.

Owns: the truth page, health and diet FAQs, the nutrition accordion, legal, anything a GP might read.

Voice B, the desi underdog

Warm, proud, slightly cheeky, family first. First person plural, jokes about aunties and shakers, allusions to desi pop culture.

Owns: hero, ticker, gifting, cart microcopy, stir gallery captions, the founder band, social copy.

Both share one spine: short declarative sentences, concrete nouns (dal, kadhi, spoonful, pot), and zero wellness jargon.

The rules that are cheap to break by accident

  • No em dashes in copy, ever. Restructure the sentence. The only exception is the attribution dash in the founder signature.
  • The word “dose” is banned. Say “how much”, a serving, or a spoonful.
  • Only three health claims exist, used verbatim from the GB register: protein contributes to a growth in muscle mass, to the maintenance of muscle mass, and to the maintenance of normal bones. Paraphrasing one is a compliance breach, not a style choice.
  • One pop-culture easter egg per surface, allusion rather than quotation.
  • Prices, shipping and discounts come from lib/pricing.ts, in integer pence. Formulation and nutrition figures come from components/shop/nutrition-data.ts. Nothing is retyped by hand.
  • British English throughout: whilst, flavour, colour, litre.

Full detail, including the change-impact map that lists every surface repeating each product fact, is in BRAND.md. Build conventions, the responsive contract and the accessibility bar are in PLAYBOOK.md.
